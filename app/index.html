How to read this view

These are past test weeks. The forecast methods used earlier sales to predict each day; “actual” sales were revealed afterward. When the source data had no row for a product on a test day, this project assumed zero sales and labels it “assumed zero.” That does not prove there was no customer demand or that the product was in stock.

The historical comparison does not use your stock inputs. The restocking panel above uses a saved future forecast and the stock details you enter; its suggested quantity is a planning estimate.
